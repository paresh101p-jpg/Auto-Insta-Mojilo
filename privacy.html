1. Introduction

Mojilo Mart ("we", "our", or "us"), located at B-404, 4th Floor, Radhaswami Appartment, Punagam, Varachha, Surat, Gujarat 395010, India, is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our applications, tools, and automated publishing services collect, use, and manage data when integrating with Meta Platforms (Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp).

2. Information We Access and Process

Our automation applications only access permissions explicitly authorized by our administrators to manage our social business presence. This includes:

  • Instagram Graph API & Content Publishing: To schedule, publish, and manage reels, feed posts, and stories on our official Instagram account (@MOJILOMART).
  • Meta Catalog & Product Tagging: To access our owned product catalog and associate product tags (e-commerce apparel, custom T-shirts, DTF prints) with published media.
  • Facebook Page Management: To publish product updates, videos, and respond to customer engagements on our official Facebook Page.

3. Data Protection and Storage

We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal data. Access tokens and API secrets are securely stored with end-to-end cryptographic encryption and are solely utilized to maintain product synchronization between our inventory and our social shop storefronts.

4. User Rights and Data Deletion

Users may request the deletion of any data associated with their interactions with our pages by contacting us at support@mojilomart.com or via written request to our registered Surat office address.
